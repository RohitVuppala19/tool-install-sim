        How the model works

        Each tool goes through rigging and setting, then electrical hookup and process piping in parallel, then closeout and qualification. A task starts when its predecessors finish, plus a handoff delay, and a crew of the right trade is free.

        Every run draws task durations from a right-skewed triangular multiplier on planned hours, late OEM deliveries, and handoff gaps. Actual craft hours are priced at burdened wages, foreman supervision is added, then the strategy's markup is applied.

        Sources

        • Wages: BLS OEWS mean hourly wages, Boise City, ID MSA, May 2025. Millwright uses May 2023, the latest Boise figure published.
        • Labor burden: BLS ECEC, June 2026, construction and extraction occupations.
        • Contractor overhead and profit: Buildern, Typical Contractor Overhead and Profit, 2026.
        • Planning assumptions: craft hours, crew sizes, handoff delays, delivery slip and duration risk. Replace them with contractor bids or historical install records.
